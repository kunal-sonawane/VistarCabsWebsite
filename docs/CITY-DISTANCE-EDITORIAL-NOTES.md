# City visitor distance notes

The travel pages display approximate road distances from a city-centre reference. They are planning guides, not live navigation results. The visitor's actual pickup point, chosen entrance, road route, traffic and conditions can change the distance and timing. Airport pages keep city-centre distance figures separate from the airport-to-city leg.

Official Maharashtra tourism pages informed several reference figures:

- Nashik district, Trimbakeshwar (about 28 km), and Pandavleni (about 8 km): [Nashik district](https://maharashtratourism.gov.in/districts/nashik/), [Trimbakeshwar](https://maharashtratourism.gov.in/temple/trimbakeshwar/), [Pandavleni](https://maharashtratourism.gov.in/cave/pandavleni/).
- Ellora (about 31 km from Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar) and Ajanta (about 100 km): [Ellora](https://maharashtratourism.gov.in/cave/ellora/), [Maharashtra Tourism heritage leaflet](https://maharashtratourism.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/MH-Leaflet-Buddhist-Heritage-Circuit-Slides.pdf).
- Shirdi (about 87 km from Nashik) and Shani Shingnapur (about 70 km from Shirdi): [Shirdi](https://maharashtratourism.gov.in/temple/shirdi/), [Shani Shingnapur](https://maharashtratourism.gov.in/temple/shani-shinganapur/).
- Sinhagad Fort (about 35–40 km from Pune): [Pune district tourism](https://pune.gov.in/en/tourist-place/sinhagad/).

Other city-centre figures are approximate editorial estimates. Recheck them against a live route for the customer's exact addresses before quoting a trip. The page does not promise cab availability, fare, journey time, visitor access or opening hours.
