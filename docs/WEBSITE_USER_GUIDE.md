# Website visitor guide

This guide explains how a visitor can use the Vistar Cabs website to plan a trip and contact the team.

## Start on the homepage

The homepage introduces Vistar Cabs and puts the trip enquiry form near the top. Scroll down to explore the Kumbh Mela guide, trip services, corporate travel information, cities, airports, destination guides, customer feedback, popular routes and common questions.

Use the navigation menu to open Home, About Us, Contact Us, Blogs and Services. On a phone, open the menu button to see the same links.

## Send a trip enquiry

1. Choose the service that matches your trip: **Outstation**, **Airport**, **Hourly** or **Corporate**.
2. Choose **One Way** or **Round Trip** if that choice is available for the selected service.
3. Enter the pickup location, destination, pickup date and pickup time.
4. Enter your name and callback phone number. Complete any relevant service-specific fields shown on the form.
5. Select **Send Enquiry**.

The website sends the enquiry to its API, which validates and stores it in MongoDB. After a successful save, WhatsApp opens with a prepared message addressed to the business. Review the message and press **Send** in WhatsApp to contact the owner. Opening WhatsApp creates a message draft; it does not send the message automatically.

If the form reports that saving failed, the enquiry may not be in the database. Try again when the connection is available, or use the contact links to call or message the business directly.

## Explore pages

- **Cities:** read city-specific travel information and follow links to local places and routes.
- **Airports:** find information for airport pickups and drops. Add flight or terminal details when the form offers them and you know them.
- **Destinations:** explore places around Maharashtra and use the enquiry form to ask about a journey.
- **Routes:** view basic information about common city-to-city trips.
- **Blogs:** browse travel articles and planning information.
- **Contact Us:** find the configured phone, WhatsApp, email and social links.

Travel information helps with planning. Confirm vehicle availability, pickup details, timings and fare directly with Vistar Cabs before travel. This website does not take online payments.

## On a phone

The pages and form adapt to phone screens. Tap a location field and type to search suggestions. If the keyboard covers a field, scroll the page until it is visible. Use the WhatsApp or call control when you prefer to speak with the team.

## Privacy

Only submit contact and trip information needed for a callback. Do not enter payment card details, passwords or identity documents in the form.
